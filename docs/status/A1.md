# A1 — Calculators
State: done   ·   Branch: wp/A1-calc   ·   Updated: 2026-10-21

## Done
All in `AbacusKit/Sources/AbacusDomain/Calc/`. Pure functions on `Calc`, no I/O. Inputs are
entities (`Account`, `Category`, `TxnWithLines`, `BudgetPlan`/`BudgetAllocation`, `Event`) plus
a `CurrencyConverter`. Outputs are value types. A transaction's day is
`Day(txn.occurredAt, calendar:)`, and every function takes `calendar:` (default `.current`).
`Txn.bookedDay` is ignored (D21).
- **Shared (`Calc.swift`)**: `RateDate` (`.day(d)` / `.latest`), `MissingRate`, `Unconverted`
  (count of amounts left out + missing rates + unknown account IDs; a rate is never taken as 1),
  `LimitStatus` ok/near/over, `Calc.limitStatus`, `Calc.fractionUsed`.
- **Effects and balances (`Balances.swift`)**, R-ACC-3, R-TX-4/5/6:
  - `mainEffects`: expense out (refund in), income in (reversal out), transfer `amountMinor` out
    of the source and `toAmountMinor ?? amountMinor` into the destination.
  - `effects(of:)`: main ± attached records on the source account, one entry per account.
  - `balances(accounts:txns:through:)` / `balance(of:)`: opening + effects. With `through`, the
    opening balance counts only from the opening day on.
- **Net worth (`NetWorth.swift`)**, R-HOME-1, R-ACC-4, R-FX-3:
  - `netWorth`: non-archived accounts only. Assets and liabilities go by `AccountKind` (R-ACC-2),
    with liabilities shown as the positive amount owed. Each account has its native and
    converted value, and an account with no rate is listed with `converted == nil`.
  - `netWorthSeries(today:monthEnds: 11)`: month ends at that day's rate, then today at the
    latest rate (12 points by default). Also `changeSincePreviousMonthEnd`.
- **Reporting (`Reporting.swift`)**:
  - `reportEntries`: the refund rule (R-TX-4, R-SET-3, D22). With the setting off, an expense
    refund or a refund line (an `.income` record in an expense category) is income under
    `CategoryKey.refunds` and the category's spending stays the same. With it on, the amount
    is subtracted from the category. An income reversal is always negative income in its own
    category. A transfer's main amount gives no entry, but its attached records do.
  - `expensesByCategory(month:)` (donut, R-HOME-3) / `categoryTotals(kind:days:)`: parents
    roll up children (`total`, `direct`, `share`, `children`, R-CAT-2), attached records go
    under their own category, and each amount is converted at its day's rate (R-FX-3).
  - `listTotals`: income, expense, net, count of a filtered set (R-LIST-5).
  - `CategoryTree`: parent, top level, children, `withChildren` (R-LIST-2 parent filter).
- **Budget (`Budget.swift`)**, R-BUD-1…3, R-HOME-2: `planInEffect` (latest `effectiveMonth`
  ≤ month), `budgetStatus` (lines per allocation in category order, an unallocated row with
  limit = total − limits and spent = other categories, totals, remaining, `fractionUsed`,
  status, `overCount` including the unallocated row, `daysLeft`), and `daysLeft(in:today:)`
  (days after today: 10 on 21 Oct, as in the prototype).
- **Events (`Events.swift`)**, R-EVT-3: `eventTotals` gives spent in the display currency, the
  same by category, the transaction count, and, when the event has a budget, spent in the
  budget's currency plus its status.
- Tests: `Tests/AbacusDomainTests/Calc*.swift`, 45 tests with requirement IDs in their names.
  `CalcFixtures.swift` holds the synthetic fixtures and a `FakeConverter` (weekend fallback,
  inverse pairs). `swift test` green (all targets).

## Interpretations (for the orchestrator to confirm or correct)
1. **Child vs parent limits:** when a subcategory and its parent both have a limit, the child's
   spending counts only under the child's own limit, and the parent's limit covers its other
   children and itself, so nothing is counted twice. "A parent's limit covers its children"
   (R-BUD-2) applies when the child has no limit of its own.
2. **Status thresholds:** over when spent > limit. Near at ≥ 90 % up to and including 100 %,
   which matches the prototype (`pct > 1` is over). A limit ≤ 0 with any spending is over;
   the prototype showed that case as ok.
3. **Assets vs liabilities** go by account kind (R-ACC-2). The prototype split them by the sign
   of the balance. The net total is the same either way; only the two subtotals differ (for
   example, an overdrawn debit card).
4. **Income reversals** always reduce their income category. R-SET-3 covers spending only.
5. **Balance through a day:** the opening balance counts from the account's opening day on, so
   month-end points before an account was opened show 0 for it.
6. An event's `unconverted` may count the same missing amount twice when the display and budget
   currencies both lack a rate.
