# C8 — Budget: widget bug, total + unallocated, display currency, form layout

Branch `wp/C8-budget`. TA v1.6 `R-BUD-1…3`, `R-HOME-2`, `R-MGMT-3`, `R-MGMT-8`, `R-MGMT-10`;
decisions D27, D29, D30, D43, D46.

## State: done

### Widget bug (editor Home 5 747 PLN, widget Home 5 747 €)

Checked every path between the editor and the widget:

- The widget never reads the plan's currency: the calculator takes each line's currency from
  `BudgetAllocation.currency`.
- Save path: a round trip through the app database (`AppSession.live`, editor → store →
  dashboard) keeps EUR and PLN per limit (`BudgetRoundTripTests`).
- Migration v5 copies the plan's v1.0 currency to its limits, so a v1.0 plan in EUR becomes
  Eating out 400 € and Home 5 747 € — as designed (C6 test).

So the widget showed what was stored; the editor showed something else that was **not saved**:

1. A new limit starts in the last used currency, so Home was added in EUR after Eating out (EUR).
2. Switching Home to PLN made the form dirty, but Save sat in the window toolbar (easy to miss)
   and nothing on the page said "unsaved".
3. Showing the Budget section again (`sectionAppeared`) or any transaction edit
   (`reloadManageScreens`) called `load()`, silently discarding unsaved edits.

Fix: Revert + Save at the bottom-right of the form with an "Unsaved changes" note (R-MGMT-10);
`BudgetEditorModel.reappeared()` re-reads the budget only when there are no unsaved edits
(otherwise just the rates); `AppSession` uses it for section switches and data reloads (a restore
or sample data still reloads fully). Regression tests: `BudgetRoundTripTests` (saved EUR + PLN
reach the dashboard in their own currencies, totals agree; unsaved edits survive a section switch
and reach the dashboard only after Save).

**Spent 0**: correct. Spending counts under a limit only by category (its own, or its parent's);
expenses without a category count under no limit. With a total set they now count under
"Unallocated" and the total.

### Total (D46)

- `BudgetPlan.totalMinor` / `currency` are meaningful again (renamed back from
  `legacyTotalMinor` / `legacyCurrency`; same columns, **no migration**). `plan.total` is nil
  when `totalMinor` ≤ 0.
- **0 = no total.** Plans saved by 1.1.0 have `totalMinor` 0: the budget's total is the sum of
  the limits in the display currency (C6 behaviour, no unallocated row) until the owner types a
  total. v1.0 plans that were never re-saved get their old total back.
- Calculator (`Calc.budgetStatus`): with a total, total spent = every expense of the month
  (uncategorised too) in the total's currency at each day's rate; unallocated limit = total −
  limits converted to the total's currency at `budgetTotalRate` (latest for the current month,
  month-end for a closed one, D30 — TA says "latest"; a closed month uses its month-end like the
  rest of the widget); unallocated spent = expenses no limit covers. Total and unallocated
  statuses are decided in the total's currency; category statuses in each limit's currency. The
  headline converts the total to the display currency at the same rate; with no rate it stays in
  the total's currency and the missing rate is reported. `overCount` includes an over
  unallocated row (as in v1.0).

### Budget page

- "Total limit" field + currency picker next to "Applies from" (empty or 0 = no total).
- Totals, unallocated and share bars follow the toolbar display currency (`setDisplayCurrency`,
  pushed from `AppModel` via the environment); inputs keep their own currencies. Share = limit
  in the display currency ÷ total (or the sum of the limits without one).
- "Unallocated" row (display currency, note "Other categories and uncategorised spending", or
  "The limits exceed the total by …" in red) and "Total in <display>" row.
- Red `trash` button removes a limit. Currency pickers in the switch order (EUR, USD, PLN, UAH).
- "Add category limit" picker A–Z; subcategories as "Parent › Child" (R-MGMT-8).

### Widget

Headline (spent of total, remaining, % used, bar) in the display currency; category bars in
each limit's own currency; then "Unallocated" (grey name unless over) and, after a divider,
"Total" in the total's own currency — interpretation of "plus the total row": the row shows the
total in the currency its status is decided in. Note under the headline: "Total in PLN · shown
in UAH at the latest rate", or "No total set · limits in … summed in … at …". Pencil button kept.
