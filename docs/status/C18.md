# C18 — Debts view: several accounts, currency switch
State: done (awaiting merge)   ·   Branch: wp/C18-debts-view   ·   Updated: 2026-10-30

TA v1.8 `R-DEBT-14`, `R-DEBT-15` (D58). Debts tab only; no import, rules, transactions,
repository or migration changes (C17 works there in parallel).

## What changed

- **R-DEBT-14, per-account summary.** `Calc.debtAccountFlows(book, debtIDs:)` (additive, in
  `DebtCalc.swift`) sums a person's record legs per account: money out (I lent, I repaid, Paid on
  their behalf) and money in (They repaid, I borrowed), in the **account's** currency (the leg's
  amount), with the record count. The person's detail shows an "Accounts" card above the debt
  cards: account, its currency, money out, money in, "N records", accounts A–Z. Records without
  an account are not in the card (they move no money on my accounts); each such record still
  says "No account" in its row, and every other record row names its account (already so since
  C14).
- **Record form account picker**: already offered every open account, grouped (R-MGMT-9), with
  the account amount field when the currencies differ — checked by a test, no change needed.
- **R-DEBT-15, currency switch.** The shell already listed Debts among the sections that show the
  display-currency switch (`AppSection.usesDisplayCurrency`) and passed the currency in; no shell
  change. New on the tab:
  - each person row (Active) shows "≈ +₴3,400" under the name — the open balances summed in the
    display currency at today's rate — by the dashboard widget's rule: only when some balance is
    in another currency ("≈ no rate" when a rate is missing);
  - the person's detail header shows "Total in UAH" (or "Total in UAH at today's rates" with
    "≈" when something was converted), the direction, or "No rate";
  - the header "Owed to you" / "You owe" totals were already `Calc.debtSummary` in the display
    currency (same calculator and formatter as the widget).
  - `DebtsModel.converter` is now observed, so the totals follow a rate refresh.
  Per-currency balances are unchanged and never netted.
- **Sample data**: Alex's EUR debt gained one record paid from the UAH Monobank account (a
  cross-currency leg), so previews and Load Sample Data show a debt on several accounts.

## Interpretations for the owner

1. The person's converted total covers **open** (not settled) debts only, at the **latest**
   rate — the same figure as the dashboard widget. A settled person shows no total.
2. In the people list the "≈" figure appears only when converting changes something (several
   currencies, or one currency other than the display currency), like the widget. The detail
   header always shows the total.
3. The "Accounts" card sums records of all the person's debts (settled ones too), in each
   account's own currency; "No account" records are not listed there.
4. Header totals have no "≈" (as on the widget); the person's figures do.
