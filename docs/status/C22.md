# C22 — Import, rules, convert on accounts
State: done (awaiting merge)   ·   Branch: wp/C22-import-transfer   ·   Updated: 2026-10-30

TA v1.9 `R-DEBT-10…13`, `R-DEBT-19` (D59–D61) on the C19 core. Payments to a person are transfers
between my account and a linked account of the person's debt; this lane gives import, rules and the
list the matching, the dialogs and the labels for it.

## Design

- **Matching (R-DEBT-13/19).** `DebtLegMatch.debt(of:in:)` = a transfer with exactly one side a
  linked account of a debt (the other is mine). `RowAnalyzer.debtLegs` takes candidates on the row's
  own account (sending side by `amountMinor`, receiving side by `toAmountMinor`, else `amountMinor`:
  `ImportStore.candidates` also returns transfers in one currency, which have no `toAmountMinor`),
  same direction (`Flow.of`), ±3 days, any description, from **another source** than the row. One
  transaction per row and per transfer, closest day first (the C17 claim order is unchanged). Also
  for Needs-review rows. Result: "Already imported", detail names the debt, plan `link` → the row's
  fingerprints go on the existing transfer, so a re-import is an exact match; undo of that batch
  removes only those fingerprints.
- Transfers to a person's account are never fuzzy duplicates (they are matched here or not at all),
  as in C17. A row on a **linked account itself** (a statement of an external account) is never
  matched: the duplicate hint of R-DEBT-20 covers it (no silent merge).
- **Import choice.** `ImportDebtChoice(personName, debtID?, accountID?, currency, amountMinor?)`:
  no kind, no report flag. `accountID` = a linked account of that debt (external offered, virtual is
  the default); nil = the debt's virtual account in `currency`, created when missing (the store, C19).
  The preview refuses an account that is not the debt's. Labels: "Transfer to debt · <person>",
  review option "Debt…". Summary lines "N transferred to debt accounts".
- **Rule action** "Transfer to debt of <person>" (stored value stays `debt`). `RuleEffect.apply(…,
  debts:)` gives the real transfer when the person's virtual account in the account's currency exists
  (else a destination-less shape: the store makes the account on apply) and skips accounts that belong
  to a debt. Rules model keeps the debt book for that.
- **Transactions list.** "Transfer to Debt Account…" (sheet: person with autocomplete via
  `personNames(matching:)`, the person's account or a new virtual one with a currency, amount across
  currencies) and "Undo Transfer to Debt" (`unlinkTransfer`) on a transfer between my account and a
  linked one. `TransactionsViewModel` change is additive: `debtAccountIDs`, `canUnlinkTransfer`,
  `unlinkTransfer`, `canConvertToDebt` now refuses accounts of a debt (the store refuses them too).

## Interpretations (owner to confirm)

1. The C19 question: a bank row matching a hand transfer is **linked automatically** ("Already
   imported", fingerprints stored), as in C17; only a linked account's own statement is left to the
   R-DEBT-20 hint.
2. Matching covers a transfer to **any** linked account of a debt (virtual or external), as long as
   the other side is my account on the row's account.
3. `ImportStore.candidates` now also returns same-currency transfers by their sending amount on the
   receiving account; this can additionally surface such transfers as fuzzy duplicates for ordinary
   (non-debt) rows, which is the intended behaviour for inflows.
4. Legacy `Txn.debtRecordID` list rendering (`TransactionListContent`, `AppModel.openDebtLeg`) was left
   as is (C21/C20 area, nothing writes the column any more).
