# C14 — Debts
State: done (awaiting merge)   ·   Branch: wp/C14-debts   ·   Updated: 2026-10-27

TA v1.7 `R-DEBT-1…8` (D51, D52). This file starts with the data-model design (written before
coding), then progress.

## Design

### Entities (`AbacusDomain/Model/Debts.swift`, new tables in migration "v10: debts")

- **`Debt`** (`debts`): `personName`, `currency`, `dueDay` (`YYYY-MM-DD`, optional), `note`,
  `countsInNetWorth` (R-DEBT-7), `isSettled` (R-DEBT-5), `createdAt`; plus two bookkeeping
  links: `sourceAccountID` (the former debt account it was converted from, `ON DELETE SET
  NULL`; lets later imports find the debt, R-DEBT-8) and `importBatchID` (undo, R-IMP-6).
  A "person" is not a row: the Debts tab groups debts by person name (trimmed, case-insensitive);
  one person has one debt per currency, balances never netted across currencies (R-DEBT-1).
- **`DebtRecord`** (`debtRecords`): `debtID` (`CASCADE`), `kind`, `day`, `amountMinor` (positive,
  in the debt's currency), `note`, `reportKind` (`expense` / `income` / nil = not in reports),
  `categoryID` (the "Debts" category, or a migrated record's original category; `SET NULL`),
  `fingerprint` (the import fingerprint of a converted transaction, for re-import dedupe),
  `importBatchID`, `createdAt`.
- **Kinds** and their effect on the debt balance (positive = they owe me, R-DEBT-2) and on my
  account:

  | Kind | Balance | My account | Flag offered (default) |
  |------|---------|------------|------------------------|
  | I lent | + | out | Count as expense (off) |
  | They repaid | − | in | Count as income (off) |
  | I borrowed | − | in | — |
  | I repaid | + | out | — |
  | Paid on their behalf | + | out | Count as expense (off) |
  | Forgive (they owed me) | − | — | Count as expense (**on**) |
  | Forgive (I owed them) | + | — | — |

  "Forgive" is stored as two kinds so its direction never depends on the order of records; the
  form shows one "Forgive" choice and picks the variant from the balance sign (a choice of the
  two when the balance is zero).

### How a record moves money on my account

A money-moving record that names my account owns one **leg**: an ordinary `Txn` row with the
new column `txns.debtRecordID` (`REFERENCES debtRecords ON DELETE CASCADE`), so balances, the
transaction list, dedupe and backups see it like any other transaction:

- **unflagged** → a one-sided **transfer** (`toAccountID == nil`): money out of `accountID`, or
  into it when `isRefund` is set. Transfers are already left out of every expense/income report
  and budget, so an unflagged record never touches them (R-DEBT-4). The only calculator change:
  `Calc.mainEffects` (and the two SQL balance queries) read `isRefund` on a transfer without a
  destination as "money in".
- **flagged** (Count as expense / income) → an **expense** (I lent, Paid on their behalf) or
  **income** (They repaid) in the "Debts" category, so it lands in the donut, budget, list
  totals and the Debts category's drill-down without any reporting change.
- The leg's amount is in the account's currency (`accountAmountMinor` in the form, pre-filled at
  the day's rate when the currencies differ, like a cross-currency transfer); the record keeps
  the amount in the debt's currency. Description = "Lent to Ann" etc., note = the record's note.
- The transaction list marks legs with a "Debt" badge; opening a leg goes to the Debts tab (the
  ordinary editor never edits a leg). Saving a record rewrites its leg in the same database
  transaction (type, account, amount, day, category); deleting the record deletes the leg
  (cascade). Rules never apply to legs.
- A record may also have **no account** (forgive; a migrated opening balance or a line that was
  booked on the debt account; or "No account" picked for money that did not pass through the
  app's accounts) — then it changes only the debt.

### How flagged records without a leg enter reports

A forgive with "Count as expense" (and migrated records that carried an expense/income) has no
account. `DebtBook.reportLedger()` turns each such record into a synthetic, **archived**
account per debt plus a zero-amount transfer carrying one attached record (kind = `reportKind`,
category = the record's). The dashboard appends these to the accounts/transactions it already
feeds the calculators, so the donut, budget and income/expense widgets count them on the
record's day under their category — with the existing refund-line rule (D22) and day-rate
conversion — while net worth ignores them (archived) and no real balance changes. Nothing
synthetic is stored or shown in lists.

### Net worth (R-DEBT-7)

`Calc.netWorth` / `netWorthSeries` take a `debts: DebtBook` (default empty). Each debt with
"Count in net worth" adds its balance through the point's day (records with `day ≤` it),
converted like an account at the point's rate: positive → assets, negative → liabilities.
`NetWorth.debts` lists them; `DashboardNetWorth` gets one separate "Debts" figure. Settled debts
are still included in the history (their balance is zero today).

### "Debts" category

Found or created on save of a flagged record: a top-level, non-archived category named "Debts"
of the needed kind (expense for Count as expense, income for Count as income).

### Migration "v10: debts" (R-DEBT-8)

Raw SQL only (later migrations may add columns to the entity structs). One database
transaction (GRDB migration). For every account of kind `debt`:

1. A debt: person = account name, currency = account currency, "Count in net worth" = not
   archived (archived accounts were already out of net worth), settled = archived,
   `sourceAccountID` = the account.
2. A non-zero opening balance → a record without account on the opening day ("Opening
   balance": I lent if positive, I borrowed if negative).
3. Its transactions in date order, with a running balance for the kind (positive change: "I
   repaid" while the balance was negative, else "I lent"; negative change: "They repaid" while
   the balance was positive, else "I borrowed"):
   - transfer own → debt: the transaction becomes the leg in place (destination cleared), same
     id, day, amounts, description, attached records and fingerprints;
   - transfer debt → own: the leg in place, moved to the own account with `isRefund` (money in)
     and the received amount; attached records that were on the debt account become records
     without account carrying their kind and category (so reports stay the same);
   - expense / income on the debt account (Saldo "they paid for me" rows), and transfers
     between two debt accounts: records without account carrying the original kind/category and
     fingerprint, then the transaction is deleted;
4. The account is **archived** (not deleted) with opening balance 0: its id stays valid for
   remembered import mappings, import batches and rules; rules that use it (account condition
   or transfer target) are **disabled**, not deleted.

Every own-account balance is untouched (legs keep their own-account side exactly), each debt's
balance equals the old account balance, and net worth stays equal with "Count in net worth" on.

### Imports

The Saldo pipeline still maps LIABILITIES legs to (archived) debt-kind accounts; the import
commit then runs the same conversion inside its database transaction, so the import ends with
debts and records, never with a debt-account balance. Re-imports dedupe on the legs'
fingerprints and on `debtRecords.fingerprint`. Undo removes the batch's records (and their
legs) and the debts it created. PKO transfers to a person stay ordinary transactions (no rule
maps them to debts).

## Interpretations (owner to confirm)

1. A money-moving record may have **"No account"** (old debts, cash outside the app); the TA says
   such a record names my account — the form defaults to an account and offers "No account".
2. Forgive is two choices in the form: "Forgave (they owed me)" (Count as expense, on by default)
   and "Forgiven (I owed them)" (no flag).
3. Converted transactions booked on a debt account itself (Saldo "they paid for me" rows,
   attached records on a transfer from the debt) keep their expense/income in reports as records
   without account carrying their original category; the form shows their flag toggle so it can
   be turned off. Income reversals on a debt account would lose their report entry (none in the
   real data).
4. Converted debt accounts are **archived, not deleted** (ids stay valid for mappings, history,
   rules); rules that use them are disabled. "Count in net worth" is on unless the account was
   archived (then the debt is also settled).
5. Debts with "Count in net worth" add to assets while owed to me and to liabilities while I owe
   (by sign), unlike accounts (by kind); totals are the same.
6. PKO transfers to a person stay ordinary transactions (nothing maps them to debts).

## Follow-up 2026-10-27: dashboard opened scrolled down; Debts half width

- **Root cause:** with Full Keyboard Access on (the owner's Mac has `AppleKeyboardUIMode` = 2), the
  window gives its first focus to a control and scrolls it into view. The dashboard had no
  default focus, so it landed on the last control on the page — "Open Debts" in the default
  layout, "Reset layout" in a custom one — and the page opened scrolled to the bottom (the Debts
  widget above the fold was then out of sight). Reproduced in a scratch home with the setting on,
  in both layouts; with the setting off the page opened at the top, which is why it looked
  layout-dependent. Fix (`DashboardView`): an invisible focusable anchor at the top of the
  content (a background, takes no space) is the default focus, so the page always opens at the
  top; Tab still reaches the controls.
- **Debts widget half width** (owner): `DashboardWidget.debts.isFullWidth` = false; the person
  rows are compact (the ≈ sum under the name, per-currency balances stacked on the right), layout
  tests updated (+ a pairing check). Snapshots checked: debts-first and default layouts with
  keyboard access on, default width (one column) and 1600 pt (two columns; Debts at its default
  last place, scrolled to with the DEBUG option `-AbacusDashboardScrollTo <widget>`).
